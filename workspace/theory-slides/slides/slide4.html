Invisible Embeddings

Theorem C.3.3. Embeddings are invisible to the language model.

If e embeds X′ into X, then for every state s′ and every sequence of future completions, the model sees exactly the same prompts whether it interacts with X′ from s′ or with X from e(s′).

The model cannot distinguish an embedded copy from the original. The only thing visible to the model is the prompt, and an embedding preserves both prompts and the dynamics of every future completion sequence.

Formally: (X′, s′) ~ (X, e(s′))   where ~ is LM-indistinguishability — identical prompt traces under all completion sequences.
